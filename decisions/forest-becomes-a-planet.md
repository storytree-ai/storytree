# The forest becomes a planet, and each read names its agent

- **Front cover of:** none
- **Full record:** ADR-0629 in storytree 0.2's decision log, storytree-ai/storytree02 (`pnpm storytree library artifact adr-0629`)

0.3's forest becomes a planet: islands on a sphere, with the project's knowledge inside as a core.

The islands sit on a sphere and the sea between them is see-through. The project's knowledge hangs
inside as a core, entered from the stories' and capabilities' shelves and lit up by agents' reads. A
throwaway prototype is the reference for the look, not a specification: 0.3's own data, rules and
capability trees decide the build.

Each knowledge read also names the agent that made it: the orchestrator, or a subagent by its type
and task. The field is filled when the harness reveals the agent, reads "unknown" otherwise, and is
never guessed. It belongs inside the MVP with the rest of the read record, because history that was
never recorded cannot be recovered later.

The planet never runs ahead of the MVP. It builds on the 0.3 forest, and its arc is queued behind the
forest's. It keeps an arc of its own, whose first steps are the owner's capability-tree reviews, one
story at a time.

Open for those reviews, not decided here: how depth treats a loop of artifacts; how a failing island is
never hidden on the far side of the sphere; and drawing replaced decisions as ghosts, which 0.3 can
find in the library's history, since it has no decision status.
