# The forest keeps 0.2's fixed view and full-size seedlings

- **Front cover of:** stories/forest.md, capability 3
- **Full record:** ADR-0642 in storytree 0.2's decision log, storytree-ai/storytree02 (`pnpm storytree library artifact adr-0642`)

The 0.3 forest is drawn by 0.2's own forest engine, ported whole. Shown it beside 0.2's, the owner
passed the look: "looks good, land this". That kept two things 0.2's engine did differently from
the forest's first plan.

- The forest pans and zooms but does not turn, as 0.2's never did: its land is shaded against one
  fixed light.
- A seedling (planned, or being built) is 0.2's full-size pine in its yellow building tint, on
  yellow ground, not a smaller tree.
