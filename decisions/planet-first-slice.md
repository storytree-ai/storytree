# The planet begins with islands spread around a globe

- **Front cover of:** stories/forest.md, capability 1
- **Full record:** ADR-0646 in storytree 0.2's decision log, storytree-ai/storytree02 (`pnpm storytree library artifact adr-0646`)

The owner approved the first three pieces of the planet on 2026-09-27: places on a globe, the
globe view, and a failing island never hidden. His choices were W2, H1 and L1.

- **W2:** fixed spots spread over the whole globe, dealt so a young project's islands already
  surround it. A story keeps its spot for good, including after retirement. The proof covers the
  first 100 historical places and islands of up to 19 capabilities. What happens beyond the last
  fixed spot needs a later book.
- **H1:** these are new books on the forest's shelves: places on Story nodes, and the globe view
  and never-hidden rule on Story node render. The knowledge core stays a separate story.
- **L1:** the globe turns and its light follows the eye at the flat forest's calibrated angle,
  preserving the baked shadows on the island in view. The flat forest keeps its fixed view and
  is one click away.

The sea stays dark. A see-through sea, diving in, cutting the globe open and the knowledge core
each wait for a later review.
