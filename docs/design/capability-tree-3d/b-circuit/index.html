Reading the board

  • Chip: a capability. Area is its lines of code; colour is its health
  • healthy unhealthy untested parked
  • Trace: one link, from the caller (right) to what it calls (left)
  • Gate: the pad where one link lands; click it for the called chip's contracts
  • A trace passing under another
  • Edge connector: links to and from other stories
  • Wrench: open work wants this capability
