Quiet roads: one piece of The library, eighteen road styles

The same piece of land and the same roads in every swatch, at the map's resting scale and tilt. Only the road changes (and, in 15-18, the borders); gates, settlements and names are round 10's. The picked link's gold has a thin dark edge here, so it holds on yellow land. Widths step with the links a road carries (1, 2-3, 4-7, 8+): the trunk from the mouth carries 12.
