Six ways to show that a session holds a capability

Today a claimed capability gets a hard band inside its border (ADR-0923 D1). Here that band and six replacements run through a claim's whole life on one clock: it arrives, is held, goes quiet, then lands or is released. Each alternative carries "who holds this" on a different channel: border, point, texture, words, time or depth.

What every alternative shares

A capability with no code yet

Today it has no territory, so nothing is drawn. In A to F a claim stakes a lot: a patch of bare island ground inside a dashed white line, with no fill and no file dots. When the code lands the lot becomes the surveyed territory with its health fill. The lot's shape is a placeholder (real territories are cut from surveyed files), so it will shift when the code arrives. The lot exists only while the claim does.

Colour

Session colours are the sessions list's own (never a green, never the amber). The three here are the shop replay's: blue, cyan and salmon. Salmon sits close to the unhealthy red and the two blues sit close to each other; both are real, so judge each mark on whether it still reads.

Motion budget

The globe draws a frame only when something changes. Arriving, going quiet and leaving are changes, so they animate for about a second. Anything that moves while a claim is simply held (breathing, drifting, waving) keeps the globe drawing for as long as any claim exists; each alternative works with that held motion switched off, which is what "Still forms" shows.

What does not change

An increment claim still draws nothing, no coast is tinted, and health keeps its three fills (ADR-0923 D2, ADR-0825 D3). The islands here are mock-ups drawn to resemble the real ones; the colours and today's band width are measured from the site's captures.

Ruled out: a wash over the whole territory

A tint of the session's colour across the land (the "tint that breathes") is the one channel that cannot be used. Blue over the yellow fill turns it grey-green and cyan over the green fill turns it teal, so the claim changes what the health colour says, which ADR-0825 D3 forbids. The same wash already has a meaning on the globe: selecting a session fills the territories it has read. A halo keeps the tint at the edge; a hatch keeps most of the fill visible between lines; both are shown above with that risk named.
