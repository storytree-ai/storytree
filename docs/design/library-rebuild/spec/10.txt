    About: Keeps each arc whole, its intent, end state and increments, and moves each increment from proposal to active to closed. It is the arc record's one writer: it creates, edits and parks arcs, and writes the waits an arc or increment stores on other work. A closed increment stays as the arc's log entry. An arc reads closed when its last increment closes, and reopens when new work is parked on it. (ADR-1242 D2)
