**What now works**
- **Staying signed in:** the login token is saved in the browser under `jwtToken` and checked with `GET /user` when the app starts. Once signed in, the navbar shows New Article, Settings, and your username with your picture. Every API request sends `Authorization: Token <jwt>`.
  - If the server rejects the token (4xx), it's cleared and you see the signed-out site.
  - If the API is down, times out or sends broken JSON, the token is kept, the navbar says "Connecting..." and you can still browse.
  - An empty reply doesn't crash the page.
  - `window.__conduit_debug__` reports the token, auth state and current user, as the requirements ask.
- **Sign in / Sign up:** `/login` and `/register` follow the official templates and go to `/` on success. Server errors appear as items in `ul.error-messages` (e.g. "email has already been taken"). An offline failure shows "Unable to connect. Please check your internet connection." In both cases you stay on the page and the form keeps what you typed.
- **Settings:** `/settings` is filled in from your account, with `''` for an empty bio or picture. **Update Settings** saves your changes and goes to `/profile/<username>`; the password is only sent if you type one. **Or click here to logout** signs you out and goes to `/`. Anyone not signed in, or whose token is rejected, is sent to `/login`.
