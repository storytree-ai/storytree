- **My Orders** is in the side menu. It lists your orders newest first, each with its number, date, item count and total, and links to the order's page. With no orders it says "You haven't ordered anything yet."
- **The order page** shows the products at the prices paid and "Total: $32.39". Another user's order, or one that doesn't exist, shows "Error: Order not found".
- **Orders survive a restart.** I ran the real server: placed an order, restarted it, and the order, its row in My Orders and its page were all still there.

**Choices I made you should know about:**
- **Finish with an empty cart** still goes to the thank-you page, as Swag Labs does, but no order is saved.
- **Testing a second shopper:** until Part 5 (accounts) is merged, only `standard_user` can sign in. So the tests add the other shopper's orders directly to the saved data to check you never see someone else's.
- **"Price paid, even after the admin changes it"** is only partly tested. Saved orders keep their own copy of the prices, but there are no admin prices to change until Part 9 is built.
- **Overlap with Part 5:** the Part 5 session owns the shared way of keeping data in files. To avoid depending on its unmerged code, Orders has its own small version for now (same folder, `data/`). It can switch to the shared one once Part 5 is merged.
