**What works now:**
- **Product page:** under the product there's a Reviews section with `[data-test="rating"]` ("4.5 (2 reviews)", "5.0 (1 review)" or "No reviews yet"). Below it, each `[data-test="review"]` shows the reviewer's name, 1 to 5 stars and the text, newest first.
- **Products:** every item shows the same `[data-test="rating"]`. The server draws it into each item, so it stays put through sorting and search.
- **Writing a review:**
  - Someone who has ordered the product and hasn't reviewed it gets the stars menu, the text box and Submit.
  - Empty text shows "Error: Please write a review" immediately. Without scripts, the server refuses it the same way.
  - Anyone who hasn't bought it sees the "Only customers who bought thisÔÇª" note; someone who has reviewed it sees "Thanks for your review!"
  - The server enforces "bought it" and "only once" itself, so posting directly to the URL doesn't get around either.
- Reviews are saved in `reviews.json` in the data folder, so they survive a restart. I also ran a real `npm start` server to confirm the page, script and styles load.

**Working alongside Part 7:** I built in my own workspace and stayed off Search. Part 7 merged first and touched the same Products page file. **Choices I made that you may want to review:**
- The stars menu starts on 5.
- The built-in users (standard_user, admin_user) appear on reviews under their username, matching the greeting.
- Without scripts, a refused empty review sends you back to the product page with `?review-error=text` in the address, which is what makes the error show.
- To add reviews to its pages, Browsing now has a small hook that other packages can use, so Browsing doesn't depend on Reviews.
