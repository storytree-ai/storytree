**Choices I made that you might care about**
- Accounts are saved in `data/accounts.json`. You can point the shop at another folder with the `SHOP_DATA_DIR` setting. That folder is left out of git, so real accounts never get committed.
- Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), never as typed. Swag Labs' own users still sign in with `secret_sauce`.
- Spaces around the name and username are trimmed, so a name of only spaces counts as missing.
- Usernames are case-sensitive, as Swag Labs' sign-in already is.
