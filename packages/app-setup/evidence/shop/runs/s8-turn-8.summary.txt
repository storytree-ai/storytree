Part 8 (reviews) is built and merged to `main`. It went in two PRs, and both passed CI before I merged them.

**What now works**
- **Product page:** reviews appear under the product, newest first, each with the reviewer's full name, 1 to 5 stars (ÔÿàÔÿàÔÿàÔÿàÔÿå) and the text. Above them is the rating, e.g. "4.5 (2 reviews)", or "No reviews yet".
- **Products:** every product shows its rating with the same wording.
- **Who can review:** someone who has ordered the product sees the form (stars select, text box, submit button). Anyone else sees "Only customers who bought this can review it." Someone who has already reviewed it sees "Thanks for your review!"
- **Empty review:** shows "Error: Please write a review" and keeps the stars they picked.
- **Checked on the server:** it refuses reviews from people who haven't ordered the product, a second review, and stars outside 1 to 5.
- **Storage:** reviews are kept in `reviews.json` in the data folder, so they survive a restart. The code is a new `review-products` package.

**Why there were two PRs**
- **#10** added the feature, but submitting a review reloaded the page.
- After it merged, I found the project's own rule (sign-in, sign-up and checkout all follow it): tests click and read the result straight away, and a page reload made the Swag Labs sign-in tests flaky until it was changed.
- **#11** fixes the review form the same way. The error, or the thanks plus the new review and updated rating, now appears without a reload. Without scripts, the plain form still works.
