Part 9 is finished and merged ([PR #12](https://github.com/HuaMick/shop/pull/12)). CI passed before the merge.

**Stock**
- Every product starts with 10 in stock.
- Products and each product page show `[data-test="stock"]`: "In stock" from 6 up, "Only 5 left" down to "Only 1 left", then "Sold out".
- A sold-out product's Add to cart button is disabled. If it was already in someone's cart, its Remove button still works.
- Finish takes one of each product off stock. If something sold out in the meantime, Finish shows "Error: Sauce Labs Backpack is sold out" (or whichever product it was) on the same page. No order is saved and the cart stays as it was.

**Admin**
- `admin_user` / `secret_sauce` signs in and gets an **Admin** link in the side menu. Nobody else sees it, and anyone else who opens `/admin.html` is sent to Products.
- The admin page has one row per product, each with its own price, stock and Save. Saving shows "Saved" in that row without reloading the page.
- I chose two error messages the requirements don't specify: a price must look like 9.99 and stock must be a whole number, otherwise the error shows in the row.
- New prices and stock show up straight away across the shop: product pages, cart, checkout and new orders. Orders already placed keep the price they were bought at.

**Testing:** all 63 tests pass, including 9 new ones. I also ran the real server on a throwaway data folder and checked the admin access, saving, the new price on Products, selling the last one and the refusal after it.

**One thing to watch:** stock is saved in `data/` and survives a restart, as the requirements ask. So if you keep running the Swag Labs tests against the same running shop, each checkout uses up stock, and after about 10 runs products will sell out and those tests will start failing. Delete `data/stock.json` (or restock from the admin page) between runs to reset it.
