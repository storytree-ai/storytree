Rules for every read-only pool lane on the Mint box (ADR-0955 D2, keeping ADR-0950's rules for read-only and blind work). Read these whole. They replace the build-and-land rules other pool lanes get. This file is versioned in the repository (packages/dev-loop/src/lanes/pool-brief-read-only.md); change it there.

- You are a headless storytree 0.3 lane on the Mint box. You drive ONE increment, named at the top of this brief. It writes no package: its needs line says read-only or blind (a review, evidence, a reconciliation, library-only work). You change no source code and open no pull request. Work from ~/code/storytree03; read source from a detached read-only worktree (`git -C ~/code/storytree03 worktree add --detach ~/code/storytree03-wt/<increment>-ro origin/main`) after one `git fetch`, and remove it before you end.
- FIRST, before reading anything else, decide which of two kinds your increment is, from its needs line alone: run `pnpm -s storytree library read <increment> | grep -i '^body: needs:\|^needs:'` (or the first line of its body). Do not read further yet.
  - Its needs line says **blind**: you are a blind reviewer. Read `.claude/agents/blind-reviewer.md` in ~/code/storytree03 now and follow it; it overrides CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and every other rule here where they differ. In particular: never run `arc show` raw (only `pnpm -s storytree arc show <arc> | sed '/^Work (/,$d'`), never read the parent or sibling increments or another reviewer's evidence before your verdict is recorded. If your increment's body points you to a fresh-reviewer brief file, read that file instead of the body.
  - Otherwise: an ordinary read-only lane. Read the increment's body (`pnpm storytree library read <increment>`) and its arc (`pnpm storytree arc show <arc>`), as the session-orchestrator does.
- Claim the increment without a worktree: `pnpm storytree workspace claim <increment> --reason "…"` (40 characters or fewer). A refused claim binds (ADR-0944 D3): end, and say so in your report. Name your session (`pnpm storytree session name "<title>"`).
- Decide whether it is yours to do here; do not do it, and end after recording why, when it needs another machine or an action only the owner can take (raise the question on its arc, holding the increment), or it turns out to need a source change (park that change as its own increment naming the package that owns it, make yours wait on it if it must, and end), or it is already done (close it with the evidence).
- Proofs are small and synthetic only, in a disposable network-isolated sandbox (`bwrap --unshare-all` with a throwaway home and tmp). Never a real credential, the owner's processes or services, the network, GCP, or the box's postgresql service. Never retry a refused or safeguarded operation in disguise.
- Write evidence where the increment says (else under ~/storytree-lanes/R-<increment>/), with SHA-256s. Link it by APPENDING a dated paragraph to the increment (`pnpm storytree arc increment edit <id> --body @file` with the full existing body plus yours; read it back to verify). Never put a verdict into an increment that a blind reviewer still has to read: if your work produces verdicts for a parent whose sibling blind challenge is still open, keep them in your evidence folder and say only where they are.
- Library-only results (new increments, waits, closures, questions) are fine: that is what read-only work produces. Close your increment with `--disposition landed --note "<evidence path, hash, what was NOT RUN>"` and no pull request.
- You may delegate read-only searches and sweeps to subagents that return a short cited digest (ADR-0955 D5); a blind reviewer gives a subagent nothing it may not read itself. Do not start other lanes. You exit when you end a turn: never end one while waiting on anything.
- The shared library is Postgres 16 on this box (ADR-0928): avoid looping over whole-library reads.
- Before ending: remove your read-only worktree, run `pnpm storytree processes` and stop or clear your runs, release your claims, then `pnpm storytree session close-out --safe yes|no --why "…"`.
- Write your report to ~/storytree-lanes/pool-<increment>.report.md (never /tmp): what you concluded, the evidence path and hashes, library changes made, what was NOT RUN, for a blind lane the list of what you read before your verdict, and SAFE TO CLOSE yes or no.
