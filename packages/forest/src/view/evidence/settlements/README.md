# Settlements as their kind's symbols, clear of the names (rollout 2, step 4a; ADR-1236 D1)

Four islands at rest, selected, drawn by the real map code on this branch, beside rollout 1's mock of the same island
(`docs/design/capability-tree-3d/q-all-islands/stills/islands/after/<story>-rest.png`).

What this step draws (contract 3.79, round 12's settlements and polish round 1's clearance): in each territory with
links a settlement drawn flat as the architecture-diagram symbol for its capability's kind (a store's cylinder, a
service's component box, a queue's cells, an index's lens, a connection's plug, a record's pages), ink on paper over a
faint casing, 3.4, 4.0 or 4.7 of The library's units across by the roads meeting there, at the island's own scale. The
whole symbol keeps clear of every name and back from every border, so of every gate. The library's capabilities have
the mock's kinds, kept by capability rather than by title number; every other island's capabilities are services.
