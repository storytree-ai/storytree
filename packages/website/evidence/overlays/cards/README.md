# Teaching cards, on the real desktop page

`capture.mjs` builds this checkout's desktop page and opens it over the eight-story forest seed with
nothing seen yet. `first-card.png`: the plan's card beside the Arcs bar, the first kind on this map.
`story-card.png` and `story-more.png`: the story's card beside its island, then with More open,
kept inside the window. `journal.png`: Help's "What you've learned", listing the two cards shown and
reopening the story's. `decision-more.png`: the decision's card reopened from the journal, its depth
with the drawing of how a decision is kept inside the globe, still at its last beat (the seed has no
decision to fire the card, so the capture marks it seen). Retake with `node --import tsx capture.mjs --retake`.
