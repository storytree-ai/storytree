# The factory switch and the factory's card, on the real desktop page

`capture.mjs` builds this checkout's desktop page and opens it over the eight-story forest seed as the product, beside
a factory holding a role, a note and a check, with nothing seen yet (the product's own first cards are put away first).
`bar-on-product.png`: the app bar above the Arcs bar, with "Your factory" at its left and the gear at its right.

factory's card beside the switch. `factory-more.png`: its More, what and why, how an edit reaches the products and a
product's own stays its own, and the decisions behind it. `factory-parts.png`: after Got it, the factory open on the glass
sheet's Library face, its role, note and check hung there, and the role's card beside the role's dot (forest 3.63). `back-on-product.json`: back to the product and to the factory again, no card shows.
